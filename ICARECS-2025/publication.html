Publications

Please find below a list of suitable journals under MAT Journals that align with the domains of Electronics, Communication Engineering, Semiconductor Technology, IoT, and Emerging Communication Systems (B5G/6G):

  • Research & Review: Electronics and Communication Engineering
  • Journal of Electronics Design and Technology
  • Recent Trends in Semiconductor and Sensor Technology
  • Journal of Microprocessor and Microcontroller Research
  • Advance Research in Communication Engineering and its Innovations
  • Journal of Electronics and Telecommunication System Engineering
  • Advance Research in Power Electronics and Devices
  • Journal of Advancement in Electronics Signal Processing
  • Journal of RF and Microwave Communication Technologies
  • Advance Research in Analog and Digital Communications
  • International Journal of AI and Machine Learning Innovations in Electronics and Communication Technology
  • International Journal of Emerging IoT Technologies in Smart Electronics and Communication
  • International Journal of Neural Systems and Applications
  • International Journal of Image Processing and Smart Sensors
  • International Journal of Modern Electrical Engineering and Intelligent Automation
  • International Journal of Renewable Energy and Electrical Power Systems
  • International Journal of Embedded System and VLSI Design
  • International Journal of Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT) in Communication Industry
  • International Journal of Digital Electronics and Microprocessor Technology
  • International Journal of Satellite-Based Communication and Wireless Networks System

All the above journals:

  • Follow a rigorous peer-review process.
  • Adhere to UGC CARE parameters and ethical publishing guidelines.
  • Are indexed in reputed databases such as Google Scholar, WorldCat, DRJI, and SJIF, ISSN.
