Call for Papers

Prospective authors are invited to submit an abstract of about 300 words length as IEEE MS Word document in the registration link.

All abstracts will be reviewed by the technical committee to assess their quality and relevance to the scope of the Symposium. All papers abstract accepted for presentation will be published in the proceedings having ISBN.

Full length paper will be published in google indexed journals:

  • Journal of Soft Computing and Computational Intelligence
  • International Journal of Digital Technology and Network Security System
